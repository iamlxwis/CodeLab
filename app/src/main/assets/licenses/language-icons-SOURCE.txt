Reimagined 2.0 language visual audit

New vectors use Devicon v2.17.0 (MIT), the pinned Simple Icons collection (CC0) and the pinned Material Icon Theme collection (MIT). The CodeLab monograms below identify languages without a suitable bundled mark in these collections. React and Sass marks identify the corresponding JSX/TSX and Sass/SCSS language variants. OpenGL and NVIDIA marks identify GLSL and CUDA respectively. The Racket logo identifies the Scheme family. The Verilog mark covers Verilog and SystemVerilog.
